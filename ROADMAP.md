**Runnable today:** `npm run dev` starts the game server and the page. Create a room on the home page and share its address; everyone who opens it lands in the lobby (player list with ready states, the room link, the admin's choice of course, vehicle, time of day and weather). Time of day sets the sky and light (dawn, midday, sunset, night with headlights and a small light on each player); rain lowers tyre grip and shows rainfall; a sandstorm adds fog. The overlay's `weather` line shows the run's conditions. The admin starts the run once every other connected player is ready; everyone then appears beside the vehicle. Esc (or the Menu button on touch) opens the in-run menu: the admin can restart from the last checkpoint, restart the course, return to the lobby, skip to the next checkpoint or refill (both mark the run assisted) and kick. Finishing shows the results (final time, conditions, restarts, respawns, coins, stars, assisted, and the campaign total once both campaign courses are finished); the admin picks Next course or Back to lobby. Sound plays from the first click or tap: an engine whose pitch follows speed and throttle, a winch whir while reeling, rain, and effects for impacts, pickups, the hook, item use, coins, purchases, hurt, downed, revive, checkpoints, a lost vehicle, menu clicks and the win; M or the in-run menu mutes it. A player who drops sees "Reconnecting…" and is put back in their slot automatically within 30 s; an iPhone that loses its 3D view reloads and rejoins by itself; a kicked player returns to the home page. Players walk (WASD) and jump (Space), board with E at the nearest free seat (the driver's seat if standing at its door), drive from the driver seat, and leave with E. After clicking the canvas, the mouse looks around from a shoulder camera with a crosshair; while seated the camera follows the vehicle. The local player's walking and jumping are predicted on the page and corrected on each snapshot; the debug overlay shows the predicted position (`you`) and the server's (`server`) while prediction is active. Behind the start stand three crates and a heavy barrier: walking into the vehicle or a prop pushes it, E beside a crate picks it up and E again throws it along the aim, players can stand on props, and the barrier slides only while enough players push it (1 with one player, 2 with 2–3, 3 with 4–6). Four players can roll a Lodgy lying on its side back onto its wheels. The hook rests on the front bumper: E there (aiming at the bumper) takes it, E at an anchor (the orange posts) attaches it, E with no anchor near drops it; Q reels in and R reels out, from the driver seat or standing at the bumper. The barrier has an anchor on its top. The vehicle has fuel and health (shown in the overlay): fuel drains with throttle and an empty tank stops drive; hard impacts and lying upside down cost health. Two jerrycans and a toolbox lie behind the start: E picks one up into the first free slot, 1–3 select a slot, G drops it, and holding F for 3 s beside the vehicle refuels or repairs by 50%. The overlay shows the run timer, the checkpoint (`cp 1/2` at the start), restarts and respawns; driving through a checkpoint's trigger records it, and the vehicle's centre entering the finish shows `FINISHED` and stops the timer. A vehicle that falls below the kill plane or whose health reaches 0 restarts with everyone at the last checkpoint, with fuel and health at least 50%, props, items and inventories as they were recorded. Players have 100 health (shown as `health` in the overlay): a fall landing faster than 9 m/s, the vehicle closing on them faster than 5 m/s, or a prop faster than 6 m/s hurts them; at 0 they are downed (lying flat, `DOWNED Ns` counting down from 30) and respawn beside the vehicle at 50, at once if everyone is downed. A health kit (white, behind the start) held with F for 3 s revives a downed teammate in front of you, heals a hurt one by 50, or heals yourself. Four gold coins float along the road: touching one adds it to the crew's wallet (`coins` line). Beside the road at z = −20 stand a fuel station (right) and a garage (left): park the vehicle in the zone and hold E aiming at the post for 3 s to fill the tank or repair it. E at the fuel station's counter opens the shop menu (jerrycan 3, toolbox 3, health kit 2 coins). After the finish the overlay shows the final time (elapsed + 60 s per restart + 30 s per respawn − 5 s per coin kept) and the stars.

## How to run and test

Requires Node 22.12 or later. Node 23 and 25 work but print an `EBADENGINE` warning on install, because Vitest lists only the even-numbered releases.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `npm install` | Installs every package in the workspace |
| `npx playwright install chromium webkit` | Downloads the two browsers the smoke test uses; needed once |
| `npm run dev` | Starts the game server on port 3000 and the page with live reload. Open the address Vite prints, normally `http://localhost:5173/`; it also prints the address for other devices on the same Wi-Fi |

| `npm run typecheck` | Typechecks every package, config, experiment and the smoke test |
| `npm run build` | Builds the client into `packages/client/dist` |

In the page: a room opens on the lobby: the admin picks the course (`airport`, the default: course 1, about 2.6 km with every segment type, one fuel stop with its shop and one garage; or the development courses `test` and `segment-demo`), the vehicle (Lodgy or RV), time of day and weather, and presses Start once everyone else is Ready. Nobody is seated at the start; press E beside the vehicle.

Portrait shows "Rotate your phone to landscape". Add to Home Screen opens the game fullscreen.

## Known issues

- On course 1 the RV passes the bank only with the winch (gravity along the 15° bank, 0.26 of its weight, exceeds its engine force at 0.20, which friction does not change; a solo driver can winch from the seat) and the roadworks only by pushing (the drive test excludes both). Whether that is how the owner wants the RV to play is for the play-through. All lengths are data in `courses/airport.ts`.
- The HUD's look and layout are unchecked by hand (step 12, owner sign-off). The e2e test `smoke.spec.ts` "two players share a room" failed twice in full runs under software rendering (fps about 13) and passes alone (6 of 6 repeats); not confirmed against the pre-HUD build in a full run.
- A braked vehicle creeps at under 0.1 m/s on the steep (25°) reference slope. The wheel model causes it; a scenario test bounds it.
- The production bundle is about 4.9 MB (1.8 MB gzip) since step 4 brought Rapier back into the page.
- An upturned vehicle or an RV on its side cannot be righted: it is lost only when its health reaches 0 or it falls below the kill plane, which restarts the run at the last checkpoint. Lying upside down costs health, so an upturned vehicle is lost after a while and the run restarts; an RV on its side with health left has no restart button until step 11's in-run menu (`restartCheckpoint()` is ready for it) (W8, owner to confirm). A Lodgy on its side is righted by four pushers.
- Reloading the page, or losing the connection, returns the player to the name form. Joining again usually makes a new player, because the server removes a disconnected player at once; if the server has not yet seen the old connection close, the same tab takes its old place back. Held slots arrive at step 11 and automatic reconnection at step 19.
- After the server discards a backlog of inputs, the view holds the newest snapshot until the clock offset converges (5% per snapshot).
- The roof is reached from a crate or the barrier (D11); nobody has checked it by hand, and no test covers it.
- A prop reset by the kill plane returns to its spawn even if something now stands there; the overlap can push bodies apart hard. A checkpoint restart sends a prop that would overlap the vehicle to its own spawn, but a prop held at the record is recorded at its course spawn and may overlap another prop there.
- Across a restart (and a kill-plane respawn) the page blends bodies from their old place to the new one over one snapshot interval, so they glide for 50 ms instead of jumping (visual, for the owner); the restart tick's snapshot still shows the last throttle and steer.
- Reviving or healing a teammate closer than about 1.9 m needs looking down at them: the crosshair rule measures 20° from the eye to the teammate's centre (owner to judge).
- A body touching a player keeps its hit cooldown alive, so a vehicle a player leans on and that then accelerates into them does not hurt until contact breaks for 0.5 s (J4, owner to judge).
- A restart before the first checkpoint gives every player 100 health and an empty inventory: cp-0 is recorded when the room is created, before anyone joins, so everyone counts as a late joiner. Step 11's run start should record cp-0 with the players present.
- A downed player below the kill plane can still be revived; the kit is spent and the kill-plane respawn follows.
- While downed, a player's name label stays above where the head would be standing.
- Within 2 m of the shop counter, E opens the shop menu rather than boarding or picking up (the spec's interact priority); standing right behind the pump and aiming past it toward the counter opens the shop too.
- The shop menu, coin discs and tapping the menu on a phone have not been exercised in a browser beyond the overlay line.
- On a restart tick, `coin`, `use` and pickup events raised earlier in that tick are still returned though the restart undoes them; only queued `buy` events are dropped. Nothing consumes events yet (steps 12 and 18 should drop or ignore events of a restart tick).
- While the shop menu is open the slot is frozen but the aim follows the look input, so on a phone a right-half drag turns the player while shopping (M9 read as "aim kept live").
- On a phone the touch Use button can show during an operate hold (it acts on items only). The HUD prompt names the Act and Use buttons on touch; the debug overlay still shows the keys.
- Rapier 0.21 sometimes answers scene queries with nothing for one zero-length step after a teleport: measured on the test ground as one skip in eight when only the vehicle moved, never on the segment demo or in 1,500 restarts. The world and the predictor now probe with a ray after settling and step again while it misses; the mechanism is not understood, so any new teleport-then-query site must use the same probe.
- A reconnect that comes back as a different player ("Your place in the room was lost.") leaves that new player as a held slot for 30 s, which others see in the lobby and which counts toward the 6.
- After a restart the vehicle settles onto its suspension at about 0.06 m/s for a moment, as it does when a room is created.
- Items restored or dropped at a restart are not checked against the vehicle's box; items collide with statics only, so one inside the chassis stays there until picked up.
- A hook dropped by a player standing on the barrier falls through to the ground under it: the drop ray finds ground colliders only (W2).
- Boarding while holding the hook stows it, but interact always drops the hook first, so that path is a guard with no test.
- A long run-up over the test ground's ramp costs the Lodgy up to 2% health on landing (impact threshold 4 m/s per tick change, a starting value).
- Dropped items collide with ground and walls only: they fall through the vehicle and props.
- A ford rock reads as "airborne" to the wheel that rides on it (rocks are walls, not ground), so that wheel uses road grip for the tick.
- A fifth pusher on the vehicle adds a little over four: the controller's contact flickers, and the cap counts the first four players pushing on each tick.
- Shove-out runs at a fixed height, so shoving uphill on a slope can overlap the ground and send the player to a spawn slot.
- A vehicle faster than about 22 m/s can lift a jumping player onto its roof (roof guard, D12).
- The client interpolates through teleports, so after exiting a seat or respawning a player's mesh slides for one interval (visual, for the owner).
- A quick double tap 16 ms apart can lose the second press because the world remembers the merged input.
- On touch, a joystick dip into the dead zone while throttling restarts the 8 s stuck window (the driver's throttle reads as 0), and can release the Reset button mid-hold (step 25 review item 8).
- With input-queue catch-up (skipped inputs), a client hold of exactly 1.5 s can reach the world as fewer than 90 ticks, so the player may need to hold slightly longer (step 25 review item 9).
