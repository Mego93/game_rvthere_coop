# Sound effects licence record

Every file is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), checked on the licence field of its OpenGameArt.org page. One-shots were converted to mono MP3 (libmp3lame, 96 kbps) and trimmed (leading silence removed, short fades at the ends).

Loops (`engine.wav`, `winch.wav`, `rain.wav`) are mono 16-bit WAV at 22.05 kHz, re-encoded from the original downloads, because MP3 padding puts a gap in a looped buffer. `engine.wav` is the source cycle repeated 4 times, `winch.wav` the source cycle repeated 2 times, and `rain.wav` is a 6 s loop cut from the source with a 0.5 s crossfade from its tail into its head.
