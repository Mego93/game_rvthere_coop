# Story mode: design notes

Status: owner's answers recorded (2026-11-18); minigame details still get short design conversations when each is built. Comes after the five stages (step 40), which it reuses. Roadmap step 41.

## Intent

Retell the group's Marrakech trip (Sunday to Saturday) as a co-op game the six friends play together, each as himself, with the main game's mechanics and the trip's moments as minigames.

## Rules (owner)

- Co-op; each player has HP, food, money, happiness and exhaustion. A downed player must be revived, else the day is lost (the day restarts; finished days stay done). HP can be lost by driving, hunger and an angry ally, so every day can be lost.
- Days aim for about 10 minutes; longer is fine when a day needs it (owner: "if 10 mins is too short, it's okay you can bypass this").
- Minigames: golf is played in turns; every other minigame is played by everyone at once with a team score.
- Money comes from ATMs. Food and items come from the villa kitchen, a small grocery shop and the big Atacadão.
- Items sold: fromage blanc (shield), Koke Zero (double damage), Treedent (speed), Nutella crêpes, tajine (heals), water, Moroccan tea, coffee (a speed boost that turns into a slowdown after a while).
- Names in game (owner): PrimeGolf, Koke Zero, Treedent; the Paris metro line 14 keeps its name.
- Vibecoding raises happiness and tires.

## Days (owner)

- **Sunday**: arrival at the airport, take the Lodgy (obstacles and enemies until the rental car stop), drive to the villa, eat tajine and discover the villa, PrimeGolf (Topgolf-like, hitting balls at targets, in turns), drive back and sleep at the villa.
- **Monday**: a buggy in the Agafay desert (a new vehicle, fast and very good off-road; an obstacle map like Need for Speed), lunch in the desert, then Marrakech: the tailor (Ishan and Sacha use the outfit picker; very expensive, $1,500 and more; the others watch, drink tea or coffee, or vibecode), the main city and the souk.
- **Tuesday**: rest at the villa, workout, pool until night; then the birthday: drive into Marrakech to find the restaurant (free exploration by car; park with a parallel-parking challenge; only one quarter, "the rich one", is built; heavy traffic and roundabouts), the restaurant with many events (QTEs to avoid giving tips), the rooftop bar, the casino (roulette-like).
- **Wednesday**: rainy; chill at the villa, then a massage in Marrakech (a dance-game rhythm minigame; failed moves cost HP).
- **Thursday**: skipped in the story (the Ourika drive exists as the main game's stage 3).
- **Friday**: wake up at 5 am for the hot-air balloon (air parkour), then rest at the villa and vibecode.
- **Saturday**: drive to the airport (as Sunday), then the ending scene with credits, like GTA V's ending C (2D airport parkour, flight and metro as part of that ending, details later).
