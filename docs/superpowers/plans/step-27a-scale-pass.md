# Step 27a: Scale pass (the character and everything at character size to 0.8)

Owner request (2026-11-15): "shouldn't the Lodgy be bigger and the char smaller (hence the items, obstacles etc.)?" Owner's answer to option 1: scale the character and everything at character size by 0.8 through one constant, keep the vehicles, roads and the course as they are, so the vehicles look about 25% bigger beside the crew. Spec: "Players", "Conventions", "Props", "Items". Code: `packages/shared/src/constants.ts` (PLAYER_*, HOOK_RANGE, BOARD_RANGE, PUSH_HEIGHT, HANDS_RANGE, HOLD_OFFSET, THROW_*, USE_RANGE, PROP_KINDS, ITEM_KINDS), `walk.ts`, `player.ts`, `holds.ts`, `coins.ts`, `interact.ts`, `seats.ts`, `vehicles.ts` (seat positions), `stops.ts`, `packages/client/src/render/playerMesh.ts`, `propMesh.ts`, `coinMesh.ts`, `models.ts`/`modelManifest.ts` (prop, item and character model scales), the third-person camera (`game.ts`), the HUD minimap marker sizes if they use metres.

This plan fixes names, rules and test cases. Implementers write the tests first. Where the plan and the spec disagree, or neither answers a question, stop and report. All numbers are starting values (feel is the owner's); the step ends at `needs sign-off`.

## Rules

Z1. One constant `CHARACTER_SCALE = 0.8` in `constants.ts`. Every constant below that measures the body or something sized for the body is written as `base * CHARACTER_SCALE` with the base kept visible (a one-line comment is not needed; the name shows it), so the owner can change the scale by editing one number.
Z2. Scaled by `CHARACTER_SCALE` (lengths only): `PLAYER_RADIUS` (0.35 to 0.28), `PLAYER_HEIGHT` (1.8 to 1.44), `PLAYER_EYE_HEIGHT` (1.6 to 1.28), `PLAYER_CROUCH_HEIGHT` (1.1 to 0.88), `PLAYER_CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT` (0.95 to 0.76), `PLAYER_STEP_HEIGHT` and `PLAYER_SNAP_DISTANCE` (0.3 to 0.24), `PUSH_HEIGHT` (1.0 to 0.8), `HOLD_OFFSET` (the whole vector), `HOOK_RANGE`, `BOARD_RANGE`, `HANDS_RANGE`, `USE_RANGE` (2 to 1.6), `THROW_HEAD_CLEARANCE`, and the player-sized prop and item boxes: `crate` (0.3 half extents to 0.24) and `barrier` (1.0, 0.5, 0.4 to 0.8, 0.4, 0.32) and the item boxes in `ITEM_KINDS`.
Z3. NOT scaled (kept by decision, stated in the decision line): every speed and acceleration (walk, sprint, crouch, slide, air accel, jump speed, throw speed), gravity, all masses and forces (props keep 15 kg and 150 kg; push force 1500 N), all times, the vehicles (size, mass, engine, seats positions), the road, the course and its segments, the winch (length, force), health values and damage thresholds, `ROAD_WIDTH`, the stop zones. The player therefore moves about 25% faster relative to its size and jumps about 1.27 m (about 88% of its height); the owner judges at sign-off. Jump height relative to the body is the number to watch.
Z4. Consequences the implementers must handle: a heavy barrier at 0.8 scale is 1.6 m wide with 1 m gaps in a roadblock (the roadblock gap rule and the gates corridor use the barrier half extents and the vehicle width; the arithmetic must still hold: gates corridor `2 * (GATES_OFFSET - barrier.halfExtents.x) >= 2 * rv.halfExtents.x + 1.0` and the roadblock gaps narrower than the RV; recompute and, if the roadblock gaps grow past the RV width, move the barrier lateral spacing in `constants.ts` so the gaps stay the same size as today, reporting the numbers); the seat door points and exit points (`seats.ts`) use the vehicle config, not the player size, but the exit shove-out and `standingSpot` clearances use `PLAYER_RADIUS`; the Lodgy roof (1.58 m) is now above the eye (1.28 m), so a player standing beside it sees only the roof edge: check the roof-standing rules (the roof guard in `world.ts` lifts a player whose feet are inside the footprint no deeper than the step height below its roof face; the step height is scaled), and that a jump (1.27 m) still reaches the Lodgy roof from the ground only by the existing rules (the spec says jumping reaches 1.27 m, the roof is 1.5 m: unchanged answer, but verify); `PLAYER_SLIDE_SLOPE_DEG`, `PLAYER_MAX_SLOPE_DEG` are angles and unchanged.
Z5. Client drawing: the character model scale is `CHARACTER_SCALE` times today's scale (the model is fitted to the player height: fit it to `PLAYER_HEIGHT`, not to a literal 1.8), including the crouch and slide drawing (the 0.6 Y scale stays a ratio), the name tag height (above the head) and the seat ring; prop, item, coin and anchor meshes and models follow the scaled boxes (they are fitted to `halfExtents`; check any literal sizes); the third-person camera follow distance and height scale for the on-foot camera only (the vehicle camera is unchanged); the first-person prototype uses the scaled eye heights automatically.
Z6. The drawn size of everything in the world that is not at character size (buildings, palms, cacti, props in the decor, fuel pump, parasols) is unchanged; only the player-sized things shrink. Pickups and the fuel pump's operate point reach follow `USE_RANGE`.
Z7. The cable sweep (`experiments/cable`, winch T8 tests) is re-run after the pass because the plan of step 26 flagged thin pitch margins: report the numbers in the decision line; the winch is unscaled so no change is expected.
Z8. The existing tests that hard-code the old sizes change to the new values or, better, to expressions of the constants. The implementer greps `PLAYER_`, `1.8`, `1.6`, `0.35`, `HANDS_RANGE`, `BOARD_RANGE`, `USE_RANGE`, `PUSH_HEIGHT`, `HOLD_OFFSET`, `crate`, `barrier`, `ITEM_KINDS`, `halfExtents` across `packages/*/src`, `e2e/` and `experiments/`, lists every test changed and why, and does not weaken a test: a test that fails because of a real behaviour change (for example a jump no longer reaching something) is reported, not edited.
