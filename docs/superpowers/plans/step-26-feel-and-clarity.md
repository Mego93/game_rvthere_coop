## Part A: sprint, crouch, slide (on foot)

M1. New optional input booleans `sprint` and `crouch` on `PlayerInput` (absent means false, parsed like `reset`: absent stays absent, a non-boolean is rejected, `NEUTRAL_INPUT` unchanged, the input queue does not OR them across skipped inputs). Keys: Shift = sprint (hold), C = crouch (hold) on desktop; touch gets a Sprint toggle button in `#touch-actions` and no crouch (touch has no slide).
M2. Constants in `constants.ts`: `PLAYER_SPRINT_SPEED = 7.0` (walk is 4.5), `PLAYER_CROUCH_SPEED = 2.2`, `PLAYER_CROUCH_HEIGHT = 1.1`, `PLAYER_CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT = 0.95`, `PLAYER_SLIDE_SPEED = 8.0`, `PLAYER_SLIDE_TIME = 0.8` s, `PLAYER_SLIDE_MIN_SPEED = 5.5` (the horizontal speed needed to start a slide), `PLAYER_SLIDE_COOLDOWN = 1.0` s, `PLAYER_SLIDE_STEER = 0.15` (fraction of the wish direction that bends a slide).
M3. Sprint: moves at `PLAYER_SPRINT_SPEED` only while grounded, `moveY > 0` (forward component positive), not crouched, not sliding, and not holding a prop or the winch hook (carrying slows to walk speed). No stamina. Air acceleration is unchanged.
M4. Crouch: while `crouch` is held and grounded, the capsule's height becomes `PLAYER_CROUCH_HEIGHT` (feet stay put) and the speed is `PLAYER_CROUCH_SPEED`. The eye height used by interact/camera is `PLAYER_CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT`. Releasing crouch stands up only if the full-height capsule fits (a shape overlap test at standing height against walls, ground and props); otherwise the player stays crouched until it fits.
M5. Slide: pressing `crouch` (a fresh press) while sprinting at horizontal speed at least `PLAYER_SLIDE_MIN_SPEED`, grounded, and not on cooldown starts a slide: crouched capsule, speed `PLAYER_SLIDE_SPEED` falling linearly to `PLAYER_CROUCH_SPEED` over `PLAYER_SLIDE_TIME`, direction fixed at the start but bendable by `PLAYER_SLIDE_STEER` of the wish direction; ends early on hitting a wall (speed under 1 m/s), leaving the ground (air time over 0.2 s) or jump (a slide jump keeps the slide speed as carry). After the slide the player stays crouched while crouch is held, else stands (M4), and the cooldown runs.
M6. The walking code stays ONE function shared by server and predictor (decision E2): `stepWalk` gets the stance (`'stand' | 'crouch' | 'slide'`) and a slide timer in `WalkState` (new optional fields `crouched: boolean`, `slideLeft: number`, `slideCooldown: number`, all defaulting to the standing state), and the capsule collider is resized in `placeCollider`. The predictor resets them from the snapshot like `walk`, `carry`, `vy`, `grounded`.
M7. Snapshot: `PlayerSnapshot` gains optional `stance?: 'crouch' | 'slide'` (absent means standing), sent for every player, validated as optional (old clients ignore it), and `slideLeft`/`slideCooldown` only to the local player's predictor reset, in a new optional pair `slide?: { left: number; cooldown: number }` on the local player's entry. Remote players use `stance` to draw crouching and sliding.
M8. Damage and falls: crouching does not change fall damage; a slide on ground does not hurt; shove-out, standing on props, pushing, interact and hold actions work while crouched (their reach uses the crouch eye height). Boarding and boarding checks are refused while sliding, and a crouched player who boards stands first.
M9. Client: the local capsule's drawn height and the camera eye lower smoothly (0.1 s lerp, computed from the predicted state, not a snapshot); remote players are drawn lower (scale the character's Y by 0.6 when crouched) and tilted back 25 degrees when sliding. The key table in `ui/controls.ts` and `KEY_BINDINGS` gain Shift and C, and the controls panel lists them (the T11 table test covers it).
