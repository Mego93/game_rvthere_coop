## Part A: sprint, crouch, slide (on foot)

M1. New optional input booleans `sprint` and `crouch` on `PlayerInput` (absent means false, parsed like `reset`: absent stays absent, a non-boolean is rejected, `NEUTRAL_INPUT` unchanged, the input queue does not OR them across skipped inputs). Keys: Shift = sprint (hold), C = crouch (hold) on desktop; touch gets a Sprint toggle button in `#touch-actions` and no crouch (touch has no slide).
M2. Constants in `constants.ts`: `PLAYER_SPRINT_SPEED = 7.0` (walk is 4.5), `PLAYER_CROUCH_SPEED = 2.2`, `PLAYER_CROUCH_HEIGHT = 1.1`, `PLAYER_CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT = 0.95`, `PLAYER_SLIDE_SPEED = 8.0`, `PLAYER_SLIDE_TIME = 0.8` s, `PLAYER_SLIDE_MIN_SPEED = 5.5` (the horizontal speed needed to start a slide), `PLAYER_SLIDE_COOLDOWN = 1.0` s, `PLAYER_SLIDE_STEER = 0.15` (fraction of the wish direction that bends a slide).
M3. Sprint: moves at `PLAYER_SPRINT_SPEED` only while grounded, `moveY > 0` (forward component positive), not crouched, not sliding, and not holding a prop or the winch hook (carrying slows to walk speed). No stamina. Air acceleration is unchanged.
M4. Crouch: while `crouch` is held and grounded, the capsule's height becomes `PLAYER_CROUCH_HEIGHT` (feet stay put) and the speed is `PLAYER_CROUCH_SPEED`. The eye height used by interact/camera is `PLAYER_CROUCH_EYE_HEIGHT`. Releasing crouch stands up only if the full-height capsule fits (a shape overlap test at standing height against walls, ground and props); otherwise the player stays crouched until it fits.
M5. Slide: pressing `crouch` (a fresh press) while sprinting at horizontal speed at least `PLAYER_SLIDE_MIN_SPEED`, grounded, and not on cooldown starts a slide: crouched capsule, speed `PLAYER_SLIDE_SPEED` falling linearly to `PLAYER_CROUCH_SPEED` over `PLAYER_SLIDE_TIME`, direction fixed at the start but bendable by `PLAYER_SLIDE_STEER` of the wish direction; ends early on hitting a wall (speed under 1 m/s), leaving the ground (air time over 0.2 s) or jump (a slide jump keeps the slide speed as carry). After the slide the player stays crouched while crouch is held, else stands (M4), and the cooldown runs.
M6. The walking code stays ONE function shared by server and predictor (decision E2): `stepWalk` gets the stance (`'stand' | 'crouch' | 'slide'`) and a slide timer in `WalkState` (new optional fields `crouched: boolean`, `slideLeft: number`, `slideCooldown: number`, all defaulting to the standing state), and the capsule collider is resized in `placeCollider`. The predictor resets them from the snapshot like `walk`, `carry`, `vy`, `grounded`.
M7. Snapshot: `PlayerSnapshot` gains optional `stance?: 'crouch' | 'slide'` (absent means standing), sent for every player, validated as optional (old clients ignore it), and `slideLeft`/`slideCooldown` only to the local player's predictor reset, in a new optional pair `slide?: { left: number; cooldown: number }` on the local player's entry. Remote players use `stance` to draw crouching and sliding.
M8. Damage and falls: crouching does not change fall damage; a slide on ground does not hurt; shove-out, standing on props, pushing, interact and hold actions work while crouched (their reach uses the crouch eye height). Boarding and boarding checks are refused while sliding, and a crouched player who boards stands first.
M9. Client: the local capsule's drawn height and the camera eye lower smoothly (0.1 s lerp, computed from the predicted state, not a snapshot); remote players are drawn lower (scale the character's Y by 0.6 when crouched) and tilted back 25 degrees when sliding. The key table in `ui/controls.ts` and `KEY_BINDINGS` gain Shift and C, and the controls panel lists them (the T11 table test covers it).

## Part B: the seat indicator

S1. The snapshot already carries which seat each player holds (the implementer reports the field name in the plan's report). The HUD model gains `ownSeat: { id: string; label: string } | null`, computed from the local player's seat id against the vehicle config's seat list: `driver` is labelled "Driver", `seat1` to `seat5` are labelled by position, from the vehicle's seat table: front passenger, rear left, rear right and so on (the label strings live in `ui/hud/seatLabels.ts` with a test that every seat id of both vehicles has a label).
S2. On boarding, a toast "You are the Driver" or "You are in the rear left seat" shows for 2.5 s at the centre-top below the compass; while seated the vehicle panel shows a small seat chip (an icon and the label). The vehicle drawn in the world highlights the local player's seat with a thin marker for 2 s after boarding (a translucent ring at the seat position, the colour of the player's colour); other seated players' names float over their seats (existing name tags are kept).
S3. The e2e smoke test checks that after boarding, `#hud-seat` has non-empty text and the toast shows once.

It covers: what changes on foot (the camera at the eye, the own body hidden, arms or held props drawn, the minimap and aim unchanged); seated (the camera at the seat head position inside the vehicle, the model interior is removed from the real Lodgy and the camper, so the dashboard, wheel and seats do not exist; either add a simple cabin or keep the third-person default); motion comfort (head bob off, FOV 90, a field-of-view lift while sprinting); what the owner's "more real, less blocky" wish needs (a first-person view makes the player's hands and held items the near-field art, which is where the low-poly style shows most).

## Part D: a stronger winch and a tutorial

W1. First measure. Extend `experiments/cable` (or add `packages/shared/src/winch.measure.test.ts`, skipped by default) to report, for the RV and the Lodgy: the steady pull force the cable gives at its caps (`WINCH_FORCE_PER_WEIGHT` 0.6 of the vehicle's weight, stiffness 150 per kg), the maximum slope the Lodgy/RV can be pulled up by the winch alone from a stop, the heavy barrier case (150 kg), and a vehicle pulled out of a 0.8 m deep hole (needed by step 28). The implementer also lists every way the hook can leave the player (range 25 m, dropped on boarding, pulled from the hands beyond the length) and says which the owner is most likely meeting ("hook is not very resistant").
W2. Then change it, with a stability test: raise `WINCH_FORCE_PER_WEIGHT` from 0.6 to 0.9 and report whether the experiment's stability limit holds (no chatter, reel speed intact: decision 2026-11-12 "winch stiffness has an upper limit set by the reel speed"). If 0.9 is unstable, use the largest stable value and report. Make a held hook harder to lose: the pull-from-the-hands distance goes from `WINCH_MAX_LENGTH` (25 m) to 30 m with a warning (the hook line goes red from 22 m), and a player knocked down no longer drops the hook (it is stowed at the bumper). These are starting values.
W3. Existing tests that assume the old values (every winch test, the scenario tests, `drive.test.ts` RV bank winch, the solo test's roadblock push) must still pass; the implementer greps `WINCH_` and `HOOK_` across `packages/*/src`, changes nothing that is not forced, and lists every expectation changed with the new number and why.
W4. A tutorial, as overlay cards in the client (no new level): `ui/tutorial.ts` with a `TUTORIAL_STEPS` table (id, title, text, the key shown as key caps: move, jump, sprint/crouch/slide, board, drive, winch (take the hook, attach to an anchor, reel), items and the use hold, reset X, help H, the checkpoint NPC once step 29 exists). It shows automatically before the first run (a localStorage flag `lodgy.tutorial.done`, wrapped in try/catch, off if storage fails), has Next, Back and Skip, and can be opened any time from the lobby and the run menu ("Tutorial"). The text is built from `CONTROLS` so keys cannot drift (a test).
W5. Tests: T1 stepWalk speeds per stance; T2 sprint conditions (forward only, grounded, not carrying); T3 crouch headroom (stays crouched under a low ceiling); T4 slide start/end/cooldown/early end; T5 predictor and server agree on a scripted sprint-crouch-slide path (the existing predictor-vs-server agreement test pattern); T6 protocol: `sprint`/`crouch` parse (absent, boolean, non-boolean), `stance` and `slide` snapshot validation as optional; T7 HUD model `ownSeat` and the seat label table for both vehicles; T8 winch stability at the new force; T9 hook loss rules; T10 tutorial table built from CONTROLS and the localStorage flag logic (pure function); T11 e2e: Shift moves faster than W alone over 1 s, C lowers the camera, the seat toast, the tutorial's Skip.
