# Step 28b: animals (goats and scooters that appear as you approach)

This plan fixes names, rules and test cases. Implementers write the tests first. Where the plan and the spec disagree, or neither answers a question, stop and report. All numbers are starting values (feel is the owner's); the step ends at `needs sign-off`. This is the second version: the first (animals as dynamic props) was rejected by the plan review (dynamic props break restart, kill plane and the snapshot rules; the full snapshot on `marrakech` is already 23.9 KB). Animals are a separate list in the snapshot, owned by a director.

## Decisions already taken (owner, 2026-11-15) and rulings (2026-11-17)

- Goats and scooters are annoying with a small penalty (owner). Ruling: a hit costs the vehicle `ANIMAL_HIT_DAMAGE` (a fraction of full health, 0.03) once per animal, no time penalty, and a hit can never take health below `ANIMAL_HEALTH_FLOOR` (0.02), so an animal cannot wreck the run.
- They appear as the vehicle nears, server-controlled, seeded (owner). Ruling: the seed is fixed per course (a hash of the course id); every run of `marrakech` has the same animals at the same places.
- Ruling: at most 6 goats and 3 scooters alive at once. Marrakech only (an opt-in `WorldOptions.ambient`, default off, switched on by the room's run world); every existing world, drive and solo test is untouched.
- Ruling: animals do not collide with players and never hurt them. Animals collide with the vehicle only (a collision group the player controller ignores).
- Ruling: scooters and goats are not damaged and are not pushed: they are kinematic, moved by the director; after a hit the animal is flagged `hit`, stops, and is removed after 1.5 s (the client draws it tipped over).
- Art: no goat model in the repo: a blocky goat (box body, head, four legs, two horns); the existing scooter mesh for scooters. Real models are a later art step.

## Rules

R1. **Route geometry helper** (`packages/shared/src/routeAlong.ts`, new, pure): `createRouteIndex(route)` returns `{ length, project(x, z, hint?): { along, lateral, segment }, pointAt(along): { x, z, tx, tz } }` where `lateral` is positive to the right of the travel direction. `project` caches the last segment (`hint`) and searches forward and back a few segments before a full scan; results are exact (nearest segment, lowest index on ties). Tests against a brute-force copy.
R2. **Course spans** (`course.ts`, `courses/build.ts`): `BuiltCourse.ambient?: { blocked: { from: number; to: number }[] }` (along-route metres), computed from the segment ranges: the first and last 150 m; every inserted terrain, hill or pit segment (the whole span); a 60 m margin either side of a stop, hazard or pit/hill group; the director only runs on a course whose `CourseDef.ambient` is true (marrakech sets it; others undefined). `airport`, `test` and `segment-demo` stay as they are.
R3. **Plan** (`packages/shared/src/ambient.ts`, new, pure): `planAmbient(seed, routeLength, blocked): AmbientEvent[]` with `AmbientEvent = { id: number; along: number; side: -1 | 1; kind: 'goat' | 'scooter' }`. A seeded PRNG (mulberry32 implemented there; no `Math.random`, no iteration over Map or Set). Events about every `AMBIENT_SPACING` (120 m) of eligible route, jittered by the PRNG, kind goat 70 %, scooter 30 %, side from the PRNG, never inside a blocked span, at least 60 m apart. `hashSeed(courseId): number`.
R4. **Director state machine** (`ambient.ts`, pure): `createDirector(plan)` and `stepDirector(state, input: { vehicleAlong: number; vehicleLateral: number; dt: number }): { spawn: Spawn[]; despawn: number[] }` with the animal list inside the state (positions in along/lateral coordinates, converted to world coordinates by the world with `pointAt`):
 - spawn when the vehicle is within `AMBIENT_SPAWN_AHEAD` (120 m) before an unfired event and the live count of the kind is below `AMBIENT_MAX_GOATS` 6 or `AMBIENT_MAX_SCOOTERS` 3; the animal stands `AMBIENT_EDGE_OFFSET` (1.5 m) beyond the road edge on its side;
 - act once: at `AMBIENT_TRIGGER` (45 m) a goat crosses straight across the road at `AMBIENT_GOAT_SPEED` (2.5 m/s) and stops 2 m beyond the far edge; a scooter crosses at `AMBIENT_SCOOTER_SPEED` (4.5 m/s) and keeps going until its lateral distance from the road centre reaches `ROAD_WIDTH / 2 + 15` (inside the 25 m shoulder), then it is removed;
 - a hit animal (flag set by the world) stops and is removed 1.5 s later;
 - removed when the vehicle is more than `AMBIENT_DESPAWN_BEHIND` (80 m) past it along the route;
 - `rearm(state, vehicleAlong)` (restart, checkpoint reset, admin skip): removes every live animal and re-arms events whose `along` is ahead of `vehicleAlong + AMBIENT_SPAWN_AHEAD`; events behind stay fired.
R5. **World integration** (`world.ts`, `vehicle.ts`, `protocol.ts`): `WorldOptions.ambient?: boolean` (default false). Step order per tick: director step, vehicle step, physics step, `afterPhysics`, hit check. Animals are kinematic bodies in a new collision group `GROUP_ANIMAL` that collides with the vehicle chassis and the ground only (not players, not props); each is moved with `setNextKinematicTranslation`; their height comes from one downward ray cast at spawn (`physics.castRay`) and is kept from the ground below at each step. New `Vehicle.damage(amount)` (health = max(health - amount, `ANIMAL_HEALTH_FLOOR`) only for this caller; the existing health paths are unchanged: add the floor as a parameter `damage(amount, floor)`). Hit check: after the physics step, each live, un-hit animal is tested against the vehicle chassis with an intersection test on the inflated chassis box; relative speed uses the vehicle's and the animal's velocities from before the step; above `ANIMAL_HIT_SPEED` (2.0 m/s) and once per animal id: `vehicle.damage(ANIMAL_HIT_DAMAGE, ANIMAL_HEALTH_FLOOR)`, flag the animal hit, emit the event. Restart and `skipCheckpoint` call `rearm`. The director's animal id counter continues across restarts (ids are never reused).
R6. **Wire.** `WorldSnapshot.animals?: { id: number; kind: 'goat' | 'scooter'; x: number; y: number; z: number; yaw: number; hit?: true }[]` (optional; absent when none). Two new event kinds `goatHit` and `scooterHit`, each with `pos` and no payload (added to `EVENT_KINDS`; parsers and `protocol.test.ts` enumerations updated). The room's id-change full-snapshot rule is unaffected (animals are not props). Old clients ignore the unknown snapshot field; the unknown event kind is dropped by the old parser as any unknown kind (state it in the test).
R7. **Client** (`packages/client/src/render/animalMeshes.ts`, new): a mesh map keyed by animal id, created when an id appears in `snapshot.animals` and removed when it disappears; positions and yaw blend like players do (the snapshot buffer carries `animals` through blend and `full`); the goat is a blocky group (box body, head, four legs, two horns, colour 0xcbbf9a) scaled by `CHARACTER_SCALE` like props; the scooter reuses the prop mesh builder for `scooter`; a `hit` animal is drawn tipped over (rotation about its long axis). `goatHit` and `scooterHit` play the existing `hit` sound at 0.5 volume and show the toast "Goat!" or "Scooter!" through the HUD toast path; every exhaustive `Record<EventKind, ...>` in the client gains the two kinds.

## Tests (numbered)

T1. `routeAlong.test.ts`: `project` and `pointAt` against a brute-force copy on straight, curved, doubling-back and single-segment routes and on the real `marrakech` route (sampled); `lateral` sign; the hint changes nothing in the result; `pointAt(project(p).along)` returns the foot of the perpendicular.
T2. `planAmbient`: deterministic for a seed, different for another; no event in a blocked span or within 60 m of one; at least 60 m apart; goat share 70 % +-15 % over the real `marrakech` plan; the count is within 10 % of `eligibleLength / AMBIENT_SPACING` (measure the eligible length from the real course and put the number in the test); `hashSeed` is stable.
T3. Director: spawn at 120 m ahead and not before; maxima hold; a goat crosses and stops beyond the far edge; a scooter leaves at the lateral limit; removal 80 m behind; a hit flag removes after 1.5 s; `rearm` removes everyone and re-arms only events ahead; two directors fed the same inputs have equal state (determinism).
T4. World: with `ambient: true` on a built straight course (an explicit course with one event, not `marrakech`), the animal appears in `snapshot().animals` at the right position, and two worlds with the same inputs give equal snapshots over 20 s; with the default (`ambient` false) `animals` is absent and every existing test is unchanged; a restart and `skipCheckpoint` with a live animal do not throw and clear it; a player walking into an animal is not blocked or hurt.
T5. Hit rule: a vehicle driven into a goat at 6 m/s loses exactly `ANIMAL_HIT_DAMAGE` health once and emits one `goatHit` (a scooter: `scooterHit`); a second contact with the same animal costs nothing; contact below `ANIMAL_HIT_SPEED` costs nothing; health never falls below `ANIMAL_HEALTH_FLOOR`; the animal is flagged `hit` and gone 1.5 s later; the chassis only counts (a wheel-only run-over costs nothing: assert the rule, whichever way the geometry lands, and state it).
T6. `vehicle.damage(amount, floor)`: reduces health, respects the floor, leaves repair and the existing damage paths unchanged.
T7. Protocol: the snapshot with `animals` round-trips; the two event kinds round-trip; the snapshot codec test enumerations pass; an old-parser stand-in rejects the unknown event kind without throwing. Size: the `marrakech` full snapshot with 6 players and the maximum animals alive is under 25 KB (state the real number in the ROADMAP), and the animals add at most 1 KB to it.
T8. Room: a room started on `marrakech` has `ambient` on (animals eventually appear in delivered snapshots when the scripted vehicle drives to the first event; use a built course in the test if marrakech is too long); the results, stars and final time are unchanged by animals; the worker's room builds worlds through the same `createRunWorld` (check `packages/worker/src/room.ts`).
T9. Client: `animalMeshes` creates, moves and removes meshes for appearing and disappearing ids and tilts a `hit` animal; the snapshot buffer blends `animals` and keeps them through a `full`; the goat group has the named parts; the toast and sound handlers cover both event kinds.
T10. Performance (a measure test, not a unit test): the director adds under 0.1 ms per tick (median) on `marrakech` with the maxima alive, the plan builds in under 50 ms, and the 22 km world step stays under 3 ms with animals.

## Existing tests that change

- `protocol.test.ts` and any test enumerating `EVENT_KINDS` or the snapshot fields: the new event kinds and the optional field.
- `constants` tests: the new constants only.
- `marrakech.route.test.ts`: `CourseDef.ambient` and the `ambient.blocked` spans of the built course; the freshness test follows the generator if the generator emits the flag (the flag is set in `marrakech.ts`, not generated).
- No drive, solo, measure or pit test changes (the director is off by default).
